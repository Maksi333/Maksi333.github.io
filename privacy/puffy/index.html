Privacy Policy

Puffy — Nicotine Stop

Android · Effective 27 July 2026

Puffy collects nothing. There is no account to create, no server to send anything to, and no analytics, advertising, or tracking code in the app. Everything you enter lives in Puffy's private storage on your own phone, and it stays there.

Stored on your device

  • Your name and quit date
  • Pouches per day, pouches per can, price per can, currency
  • The reasons you picked for quitting
  • Check-ins, cravings you beat, and slips — with the trigger and note you choose to add
  • Your goals, their prices, and any photo you attach
  • XP, level, badges, streaks, and game scores

Never collected

  • No account, sign-in, or email address
  • No advertising and no advertising identifiers
  • No analytics, crash reporting, or tracking SDKs
  • No location, contacts, microphone, or camera access
  • No selling or sharing of your data — there is nothing to sell
  • No network connections: Puffy never talks to a server

01Who is responsible

Puffy — Nicotine Stop is built and published by Simon Nørgaard Andersen, an independent developer based in Denmark. There is no company behind it, no team, and no third party with access to the app's data.

Questions about this policy: Simon_n_andersen@live.dk.

02What Puffy stores, and where

Everything you enter is written to a database and a small settings store inside Puffy's private app storage on your device. On Android that area is readable only by Puffy itself — other apps cannot open it, and it is never transmitted anywhere.

That data is the list under Stored on your device above. Figures like money saved, pouches avoided, and time since you quit are not stored at all; they are recalculated from your quit date every time a screen is drawn.

Puffy has no user accounts, so none of it is tied to an identity. The name you enter is used to greet you on the home screen and in your reminders, and nowhere else.

03What Puffy never collects

Puffy contains no analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising, and no third-party tracking libraries of any kind. It does not request location, contacts, the microphone, or the camera. It does not read your gallery, your files, or any other app's data.

Because nothing is collected, there is nothing to sell, share, rent, or hand to a partner, and no profile of you exists anywhere. This matches Puffy's Data safety declaration on Google Play: no data collected, no data shared.

04Permissions, and why each one exists

PermissionWhy Puffy asks for it
Notifications Delivers the reminders you switch on in Settings. Every notification is created and shown by your own device.
Run at startup Re-registers your scheduled reminders after the phone restarts. Without it, reminders stop after a reboot.
Photos Puffy asks for no storage or photo permission at all. When you tap to add a photo to a goal, Android's own picker hands Puffy the single image you chose — Puffy cannot browse your gallery.
Network Declared by the app framework Puffy is built on, and unused. Puffy makes no network connections.

Notifications are the only permission Puffy ever prompts for, and you can decline it — the reminders simply never appear. Everything else in the app works exactly the same either way.

05Notifications and home-screen widgets

If you turn them on, Puffy schedules a morning check-in reminder, a weekly summary, and milestone alerts. These are local notifications: your device builds and shows them from data already on your device. No message passes through a server and no push service is involved. Each type has its own switch in Settings.

Puffy's home-screen widgets work the same way — they read your local data and draw your day count, money saved, and milestone progress. Worth knowing: anyone who can see your home screen can see those numbers. Remove the widget if you would rather they weren't on display.

06Photos you add to goals

When you attach a photo to a savings goal, Puffy copies that one image into its private app storage, so the goal still shows it if you later move or delete the original. The copy is never uploaded anywhere. Deleting the goal or uninstalling Puffy removes it.

07Exporting your data

Settings includes Export my data (CSV). Puffy writes the file and hands it to Android's share sheet, where you decide what happens next — save it, email it, or send it to another app.

Once you share that file, the copy is outside Puffy's control and is governed by whatever app or service you sent it to. Nothing is ever exported unless you ask for it.

08Device backup

Puffy allows Android's built-in backup. If you have device backup switched on, Android may include Puffy's data in the backup it makes to your own Google account. That is done by Android and Google, not by Puffy, and I have no access to it.

To prevent it, turn backup off for your device — or for Puffy specifically — under Settings → Google → Backup on your phone.

09Deleting your data

Two ways, both immediate:

  • In Puffy, open Settings → Reset all progress. This clears your profile, history, goals, and badges.
  • Uninstall Puffy. Android deletes its private storage along with it.

If you have used device backup, delete the backup from your Google account too — otherwise a copy can remain there.

10Children

Puffy is a tool for adults who want to stop using nicotine. It is not directed at children, and it is not designed or marketed for them. It collects no data from anyone, children included.

11Health

Puffy is a habit-tracking and motivation app. It is not a medical device and it does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. The recovery timeline shows general, widely published milestones — it is not a measurement of your body. For medical guidance, speak to a doctor or a cessation service.

12Your rights under the GDPR

Rights such as access, correction, erasure, and portability apply to personal data held by a controller. Puffy transmits nothing and I receive nothing, so I hold no personal data about you — there is nothing for me to look up, correct, or delete on your behalf.

You exercise the equivalent control directly in the app: view and edit everything in Settings, take it with you as a CSV export, and erase it with Reset all progress or by uninstalling.

13Changes to this policy

If Puffy ever changes what it does with data, this page is updated before that change ships, and the effective date at the top changes with it. Meaningful changes are also noted in the app's release notes on Google Play.
